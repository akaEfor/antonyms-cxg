These are the pairs generated by running full-vocabulary tests for 12 different constructions, each in both X and Y configurations
i.e. where the vocabulary seed word is in the X or Y slot and the <mask> is in the other e.g. whether X or <mask> / whether <mask> or Y

The pairs have been hand-annotated - the primary annotation field of interest is NymType (captures the contrast type).

Annotation was done initially for the `whether` construction. Pairs were fully annotated for contrast type and part-of-speech (POS) and this was copied to other construction files that had pairs overlapping with the
pairs found by `whether`. Remaining pairs in other constructions were annotated for contrast type and, in the case of antonyms, were annotated for POS.
